Sign-in security

Enforced through Firestore, so these follow the account on every device — not just this browser.

  • Login lockout: 5 wrong passwords locks that account for 10 minutes. The countdown is stored on the account, so refreshing, clearing browser data, using a private window or another device does not reset it.
  • Login activity: every sign-in, failed attempt and lockout is recorded with its date, time, device, IP address and approximate location.
  • Idle check: after 10 minutes of no activity, staff are asked to confirm they’re still there or get signed out automatically.
